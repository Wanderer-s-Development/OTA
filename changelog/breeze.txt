WandererOS 1.0 (breeze)
Release Date: 15.11.2026
================================

- Initial official release of WandererOS 1.0 based on Android 17 (Baklava)
- UI & Smoothness: Locked 120Hz display refresh rate, SurfaceFlinger pacing, eliminated GPU background blurs for 120fps Quick Settings
- Auto-Confirm PIN: Unlock 4-digit PIN immediately without pressing Enter
- Hotspot: Added Connected Devices manager, client connection limit, client block/unblock, and Hidden SSID toggle
- Network: Added LTE Carrier Aggregation toggle in Mobile Network Settings
- Play Integrity & Spoofing: Integrated AxionAOSP Play Integrity architecture with KeyboxDataPreference, target package cloaking, PIF repository fetcher, and Google Photos unlimited backup
- Preinstalled Google Apps: Integrated GooglePhotos (Pixel 8) and Velvet (Google Search & Assistant)
- Stock Launcher: Trebuchet (Launcher3QuickStep) preserved with smooth gesture navigation
- Hardware: Full support for 108MP camera, Consumer IR blaster, side-mounted fingerprint, and 5030mAh battery charging control
